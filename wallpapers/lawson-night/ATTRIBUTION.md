# Lawson Night wallpaper folder

- `wallhaven-1k6ljv.jpg`: official Lawson Night theme wallpaper, from [phuclh/omarchy-lawson-night-theme](https://github.com/phuclh/omarchy-lawson-night-theme).
- `mount-fuji-satta-pass-night.jpg`: “Mount Fuji from Satta Pass at night” by Alpsdake, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [Wikimedia Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Satta_Pass_at_night.jpg).
- `mount-fuji-kawaguchiko-night.jpg`: “Mount Fuji at night, Kawaguchiko” by Sergiy Galyonkin, [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/), [Wikimedia Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_at_night,_Kawaguchiko.jpg).
- `japan-night-cloudpunk-city.jpg`: Cloudpunk night city, downloaded from [Wallhaven 7p5jl3](https://wallhaven.cc/w/7p5jl3).
- `hong-kong-neon-mist-night.jpg`: Hong Kong neon skyline in mist, downloaded from [Wallhaven eo28l8](https://wallhaven.cc/w/eo28l8).
- `call-of-the-night-vending-machine-4k.jpg`: Call of the Night vending-machine scene, downloaded from [Wallhaven 1p92yg](https://wallhaven.cc/w/1p92yg).

The extra images are supplemental choices and are not part of the official Lawson Night theme. Wallhaven notes that images remain the property of their original owners; no open reuse license is listed on these source pages.
